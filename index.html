💡 7. Business Improvement Suggestions

  1. Start a Loyalty Programme: Give points on every purchase that customers can use for discounts, so they have a reason to return.
  2. Improve Google Business Presence: Keep timings, photos, offers and contact details updated so nearby shoppers can find the store easily.
  3. Build a WhatsApp Customer Group: Share weekly offers, new arrivals and reminders directly with regular customers.
  4. Collect Customer Reviews: Ask happy customers to leave genuine feedback on Google and social media.
  5. Use Short Videos: Show fresh stock, offers, recipes and store cleanliness through Reels.
  6. Make Ordering Easy: Offer WhatsApp ordering and home delivery for busy customers and senior citizens.
  7. Run In-Store and Online Contests: Hold lucky draws, festival offers and photo contests to keep customers interacting.
  8. Personalise Offers: Use purchase history to send relevant deals, such as discounts on items a customer buys often.
  9. Study Customer Feedback: Regularly check opinions about product range, pricing, staff behaviour and delivery.
  10. Measure Digital Results: Track reach, engagement, enquiries, loyalty sign-ups, repeat visits and online orders to see which activities work best.
